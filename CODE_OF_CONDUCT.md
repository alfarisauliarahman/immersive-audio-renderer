# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Immersive Audio Renderer aims to maintain a technically rigorous, welcoming, and harassment-free project environment. Everyone participating in project discussions, issues, reviews, contributions, and other official spaces is expected to follow this policy regardless of experience, identity, background, or role.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas, evidence, code, and claims rather than people.
- Distinguish verified behavior from assumptions and clearly label uncertainty.
- Accept corrections when technical or licensing evidence changes a conclusion.
- Protect private information, unpublished media, credentials, and commercial masters.
- Give appropriate credit for code, research, test material, and documentation.
- Help keep discussions relevant and actionable.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, stalking, or targeted intimidation.
- Personal attacks, insults, sexualized content, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberately distributing malware, secrets, private conversations, or copyrighted masters through project channels.
- Misrepresenting third-party work, project capabilities, renderer certification, or source provenance.
- Repeatedly disrupting technical discussion after a maintainer asks for a change in behavior.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, project-controlled communication channels, and public situations where someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems privately to the maintainers through the repository's private reporting/contact facilities. If no private channel is available, open a minimal issue requesting private contact without describing sensitive personal details publicly.

Reports will be reviewed as confidentially as practical. Retaliation against a reporter or participant in an investigation is prohibited.

Security vulnerabilities should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not the conduct-reporting path.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, request a correction or apology, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant. The response should be proportionate to severity, impact, context, and repeated behavior.

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this policy in official project spaces. They must avoid conflicts of interest and should ask another maintainer to review a report when impartial handling is not possible.
